Retro Metro Cricket - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Summary

Retro Metro Cricket is a single-player cricket game. It does not collect, share or sell any personal information, and it shows no ads.

What the game stores

Your team choices, settings, match history, tournament progress and local leaderboard are saved only on your own device. The game has no accounts and no servers, and it does not send this data anywhere. Uninstalling the game removes it.

What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or advertising IDs. The game has no analytics or tracking that leaves your device and uses no third-party advertising or analytics services.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if ads are added in a later version, this page will be updated first and the store listing will say so.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/avinashbn2/retro-metro-cricket-site.
